## 1. Business Overview

**What we do:** Lesedi NovaTech Hub runs free IT education programs for rural South African youth and offers affordable professional digital services (web design, branding, cybersecurity, AI, and more) to businesses.

**Two revenue streams:**
1. **Programs** — Free for participants; funded by sponsorships and grants
2. **Services** — Paid digital services for SMEs and individuals

**Current position:**
- 10+ students trained across 5 communities
- 7 months operational
- Website live, contact form working, social media active

---

## 2. Target Audiences

### Audience A — Program Participants
- **Who:** Youth aged 15–30 in rural and semi-urban South Africa
- **Pain point:** No clear path into tech careers, limited resources, no guidance
- **Where to reach them:** Community WhatsApp groups, schools, churches, local radio, word of mouth, Instagram
- **Message:** "Free tech training, no experience needed — just bring yourself"

### Audience B — Digital Services Clients
- **Who:** Small business owners, entrepreneurs, spaza shop owners, startups, professionals wanting to go digital
- **Pain point:** Can't afford big agencies; don't know where to start online
- **Where to reach them:** Facebook, WhatsApp Business groups, LinkedIn, local business networks, referrals
- **Message:** "Professional digital services at prices that work for you"

### Audience C — Sponsors & Donors
- **Who:** Corporate CSI managers, foundations, government agencies, tech companies
- **Pain point:** Need credible, measurable youth development projects for CSI spend
- **Where to reach them:** LinkedIn, direct email, networking events, conferences
- **Message:** "Your investment reaches real youth in real communities — with proof"

## 5. Channel Strategy

### 5.1 Instagram — Primary Social Channel
**Handle:** @lesedinovatech_hub  
**Goal:** Build community, attract participants, demonstrate expertise

**Content pillars (rotate weekly):**
1. **Educate** — Tech tips, digital literacy facts, "did you know" posts
2. **Inspire** — Youth success stories, founder story, behind-the-scenes
3. **Promote** — Program enrolments, service offerings, limited-time packages
4. **Engage** — Polls, Q&As, "comment your answer" posts, reposts from community

**Posting schedule:**
- Feed posts: 4× per week (Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat)
- Stories: Daily (7 days)
- Reels: 2× per week (highest reach)

**Reel ideas:**
- "5 free tech skills you can learn right now"
- "How we built a website for under R1,000"
- "What does a cybersecurity consultant actually do?"
- "Day in my life as a founder + tech educator"
- "Before & after: client website transformation"

**Growth tactics:**
- Use location tags: Limpopo, Mpumalanga, KwaZulu-Natal, etc.
- Hashtags: #SouthAfricanYouth #DigitalSkills #TechForGood #RuralYouth #LearnToCode #SouthAfricanStartup #BlackWomenInTech #TechEducation #YouthEmpowerment
- Collaborate with other youth-focused accounts
- Respond to every comment and DM within 24 hours

## 7. Word-of-Mouth & Community Strategy

Word of mouth is your most powerful channel at this stage — prioritise it.

**Tactics:**
- **Referral programme:** Every participant who refers a friend gets a certificate of recognition or a small data bundle
- **School outreach:** Write to school principals in target areas — offer to run a free 1-hour "Intro to Tech" session
- **Church & community hall partnerships:** These are trusted gathering points in rural areas
- **Local radio:** Community radio stations (e.g. Thobela FM, Phalaphala FM, Ligwalagwala FM) often interview youth entrepreneurs for free — pitch your story
- **Newspaper features:** Approach local newspapers and Sowetan/Daily Sun with your story — "Young woman bridges digital divide in rural SA" is a compelling headline

## 9. Partnership & PR Strategy

**Media pitch angle:**
> "A young Black woman from rural South Africa, armed with a BSc IT degree and recognition from FNB and Study Trust, is building a free tech school for youth who have no other path into the digital economy — and she's just getting started."

**Where to pitch:**
- Destiny Magazine / Destiny Man
- Daily Maverick (Maverick Citizen section)
- Mail & Guardian (education / technology)
- TechCentral.co.za
- Ventureburn.com (SA startup news)
- SABC News / eNCA (human interest story)

**How to pitch:**
1. Write a 3-paragraph email: the hook (the story), the facts (stats + awards), the ask (interview or feature)
2. Include your photo, website link, and a quote from a student if possible
3. Follow up once after 5 business days if no response
