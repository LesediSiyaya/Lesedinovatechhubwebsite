## 1. Executive Summary

Lesedi NovaTech Hub is a youth-led technology education initiative based in South Africa, dedicated to closing the digital divide between rural and urban communities. Founded by Lesedi Siyaya — a BSc Information Technology graduate sponsored by HP Trust and recognised by both Study Trust (Certificate of Mentorship) and First National Bank (Certificate of Service) — the Hub delivers free IT programs and affordable professional digital services to underserved youth.

We are seeking sponsorship partners who share our belief that access to technology education is a fundamental right, not a privilege.

---

## 2. The Problem We Solve

South Africa's rural youth face a compounding disadvantage:

- **Limited access** to computers, internet, and digital tools
- **No pathway** into the technology sector from rural areas
- **Skills gap** — employers demand digital literacy; rural graduates cannot meet it
- **Gender disparity** — young women are disproportionately excluded from tech careers

Without intervention, this cycle deepens inequality and slows national economic growth. Technology education is the most direct lever to break it.

---

## 3. Our Solution

Lesedi NovaTech Hub delivers **free, structured IT programs** to rural youth, covering:

| Program | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Coding Bootcamp | Practical programming fundamentals |
| Cybersecurity Awareness | Personal and business digital safety |
| Digital Literacy | Essential computer and internet skills |
| AI & Machine Learning | Introduction to emerging technologies |
| Social Media Marketing | Building an online presence for business |

All programs are delivered in accessible, community-facing formats — no prior experience required.
